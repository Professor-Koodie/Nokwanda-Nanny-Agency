Agency Policies

Our role

Nokwanda Nanny Agency connects families with childcare and housekeeping providers. We arrange introductions and facilitate interviews between the parties. We are an agency, not a recruitment company, and we do not act as the employer of any provider.

The family and the agency share the appointment decision. Families remain responsible for confirming suitability and agreeing final working arrangements.

Background checks and safeguarding

We conduct and verify background checks where available and use interviews as part of our agency process. Checks are not a guarantee of future conduct or suitability. Families should complete their own references, identity checks, and legally required checks before an appointment.

Fees and refunds

Pricing is provided on request. Call +27 61 492 9047 for current fees and a quote. All agency, consultation, and introduction fees are non-refundable once paid.

Privacy

Information sent through our contact form is used to respond to enquiries and arrange agency services. Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless necessary.
